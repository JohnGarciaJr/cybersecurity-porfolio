# Data Classification Standard

## 1. Purpose
The purpose of this Data Classification Standard is to ensure that all organizational data is identified, categorized, and protected according to its sensitivity, value, and regulatory requirements. Proper classification enables consistent handling, storage, transmission, and disposal of information.

## 2. Scope
This standard applies to:
- All employees, contractors, and third parties
- All data created, stored, processed, or transmitted using company systems
- All physical and digital information assets

## 3. Classification Levels
The organization classifies data into four levels:

---

### **3.1 Public**
**Description:**  
Information intended for public release with no risk to the organization if disclosed.

**Examples:**  
- Public website content  
- Marketing materials  
- Published reports  

**Handling Requirements:**  
- No special restrictions  
- May be shared freely  

---

### **3.2 Internal**
**Description:**  
Information intended for internal use only. Unauthorized disclosure may cause minor operational impact.

**Examples:**  
- Internal emails  
- Team procedures  
- Non-sensitive project documentation  

**Handling Requirements:**  
- Store in approved internal systems  
- Do not share externally without authorization  
- Dispose of using standard deletion methods  

---

### **3.3 Confidential**
**Description:**  
Sensitive information that could cause harm to the organization, employees, or customers if disclosed.

**Examples:**  
- Employee records  
- Non-public financial data  
- Internal security documentation  
- Customer contact information  

**Handling Requirements:**  
- Store only in approved secure systems  
- Encrypt during transmission  
- Share only with authorized personnel  
- Dispose of using secure deletion or shredding  

---

### **3.4 Restricted**
**Description:**  
Highly sensitive information requiring the strongest protections. Unauthorized disclosure could cause significant financial, legal, or reputational damage.

**Examples:**  
- Authentication credentials  
- Payment card data  
- Protected health information (PHI)  
- Security keys or certificates  
- Incident response data  

**Handling Requirements:**  
- Encryption required at rest and in transit  
- Access limited to a strict need-to-know basis  
- Multi-factor authentication required  
- Must not be stored on personal devices  
- Secure disposal required (shredding, secure wipe)  

---

## 4. Data Handling Requirements by Classification

| Classification | Storage | Transmission | Access | Disposal |
|----------------|---------|--------------|--------|----------|
| Public | No restrictions | No restrictions | Anyone | Standard deletion |
| Internal | Approved internal systems | Internal channels | Employees | Standard deletion |
| Confidential | Secure systems only | Encrypted | Authorized personnel | Secure deletion |
| Restricted | Encrypted storage only | Encrypted | Strict need-to-know | Secure wipe/shredding |

---

## 5. Roles and Responsibilities
- **All Users:** Follow classification and handling requirements  
- **Managers:** Ensure teams understand and apply this standard  
- **IT & Security:** Implement technical controls and monitor compliance  
- **Data Owners:** Assign classification levels to their data  

---

## 6. Review and Updates
This standard must be reviewed annually or when significant changes occur in regulatory, business, or security requirements.
