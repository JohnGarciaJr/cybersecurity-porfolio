# Governance Documentation

## Purpose
The Governance section establishes the foundational policies, standards, and awareness materials that guide how the organization protects information, manages risk, and ensures responsible behavior across all teams. These documents define expectations, set direction, and support compliance with security and regulatory requirements.

## Contents

### **1. Information Security Policy**
Defines the organization’s overarching security principles, responsibilities, and expectations for protecting information assets.

### **2. Acceptable Use Policy (AUP)**
Outlines appropriate and prohibited use of company systems, devices, and data to reduce operational and security risks.

### **3. Data Classification Standard**
Provides a structured method for categorizing data based on sensitivity and defines handling requirements for each classification level.

### **4. Security Awareness One‑Pager**
Summarizes essential security best practices for employees, helping reduce human‑related risks such as phishing and data mishandling.

### **5. Business Continuity & Disaster Recovery (BC/DR) Overview**
Explains the organization’s high‑level approach to maintaining operations and restoring systems during disruptions. Connects governance documents to continuity planning and the BIA in the Risk folder.

## How This Section Fits Into GRC
Governance sets the tone and direction for the entire GRC program. These documents support:

- Risk assessments  
- Compliance audits  
- Continuity planning  
- Employee training  
- Vendor evaluations  

They form the foundation for the Risk and Compliance sections of this portfolio.
