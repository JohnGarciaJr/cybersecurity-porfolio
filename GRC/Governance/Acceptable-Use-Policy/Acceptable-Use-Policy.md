# Acceptable Use Policy (AUP)

## 1. Purpose
The purpose of this Acceptable Use Policy (AUP) is to define acceptable and unacceptable use of the organization’s information systems, networks, devices, and data. This policy is designed to protect the confidentiality, integrity, and availability of company resources and ensure responsible behavior by all users.

## 2. Scope
This policy applies to:
- All employees, contractors, interns, and third parties
- All company-owned or company-managed devices
- All systems, networks, applications, and data owned or operated by the organization
- Any personal devices used to access company resources (BYOD)

## 3. Acceptable Use
Users must:
- Access systems only for authorized business purposes  
- Protect login credentials and never share passwords  
- Use company-approved tools and software  
- Follow all security policies, including data handling and classification requirements  
- Report suspected security incidents immediately  
- Maintain physical security of devices and sensitive information  
- Use encryption when handling confidential or restricted data  

## 4. Unacceptable Use
The following activities are strictly prohibited:
- Unauthorized access to systems, data, or accounts  
- Sharing login credentials or using another person’s credentials  
- Installing unapproved software or disabling security controls  
- Accessing, storing, or transmitting illegal, offensive, or inappropriate content  
- Using company resources for personal financial gain or non-business activities  
- Attempting to bypass security controls, monitoring, or filtering  
- Engaging in activities that could harm systems, such as malware testing or network scanning  
- Connecting unauthorized devices to the corporate network  

## 5. Data Handling Requirements
Users must:
- Follow the Data Classification Standard when storing, transmitting, or sharing data  
- Use approved storage locations for confidential or restricted information  
- Avoid sending sensitive data through unencrypted channels  
- Dispose of sensitive information securely (e.g., shredding, secure deletion)

## 6. Email and Communication Standards
Users must:
- Use company email for business purposes only  
- Avoid clicking suspicious links or opening unexpected attachments  
- Refrain from sending confidential data to personal email accounts  
- Communicate professionally and respectfully at all times  

## 7. Internet Usage
Users may use the internet for limited personal use as long as it:
- Does not interfere with job performance  
- Does not violate any laws or company policies  
- Does not consume excessive bandwidth or resources  

Prohibited internet activities include:
- Accessing inappropriate or illegal websites  
- Downloading pirated or unauthorized content  
- Using anonymizers, VPNs, or proxies to bypass security controls  

## 8. Remote Work Requirements
Users working remotely must:
- Use company-approved VPN or secure access methods  
- Ensure home networks are protected with strong passwords  
- Prevent unauthorized individuals from viewing or accessing company data  
- Lock screens when away from devices  

## 9. Monitoring and Privacy
The organization reserves the right to:
- Monitor network traffic, system activity, and device usage  
- Audit compliance with this policy  
- Investigate suspected violations  

Users should have no expectation of privacy when using company systems.

## 10. Violations
Violations of this policy may result in:
- Revocation of system access  
- Disciplinary action up to and including termination  
- Legal action if applicable  

## 11. Acknowledgment
All users must acknowledge that they have read, understand, and agree to comply with this Acceptable Use Policy.
