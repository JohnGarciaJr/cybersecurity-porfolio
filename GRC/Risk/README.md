# Risk Management Documentation

## Purpose
The Risk section contains tools, templates, and analyses used to identify, evaluate, and prioritize risks across the organization. These artifacts demonstrate the ability to assess threats, measure impact, and support informed decision‑making.

## Contents

### **1. Risk Register**
A centralized log of identified risks, including likelihood, impact, owners, and mitigation strategies.

### **2. Sample Risk Assessment**
A structured example showing how individual risks are evaluated and documented using a consistent methodology.

### **3. Risk Heat Map**
A visual representation of risk severity based on likelihood and impact, supporting prioritization and reporting.

### **4. Vendor Risk Assessment Template**
A standardized template for evaluating third‑party vendors, their controls, and their impact on organizational risk.

### **5. Business Impact Analysis (BIA) Template**
Identifies critical business functions, evaluates the impact of disruptions, and defines RTO/RPO values. Supports Business Continuity and DR planning in the Governance folder.

## How This Section Fits Into GRC
Risk management connects Governance and Compliance by:

- Translating policies into measurable risk  
- Informing continuity and recovery planning  
- Supporting audit readiness  
- Guiding control implementation  
- Prioritizing remediation efforts  

This section demonstrates a complete, practical risk management workflow.
