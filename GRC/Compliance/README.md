# Compliance Documentation

## Purpose
The Compliance section contains framework mappings, audit tools, and evidence‑aligned documentation that demonstrate how the organization meets regulatory, contractual, and industry‑standard requirements. These artifacts show the ability to interpret controls, prepare for audits, and maintain compliance posture.

## Contents

### **1. NIST CSF Mapping & Gap Assessment**
Maps organizational controls to the NIST Cybersecurity Framework and identifies areas for improvement.

### **2. SOC 2 Trust Services Criteria Mapping**
Aligns existing controls with SOC 2 requirements across Security, Availability, Confidentiality, Processing Integrity, and Privacy.

### **3. ISO 27001 Annex A Mapping**
Maps organizational controls to ISO 27001:2022 Annex A, covering organizational, people, physical, and technological controls.

### **4. Audit Checklist**
A structured checklist used to prepare for internal or external audits, collect evidence, and validate control effectiveness.

## How This Section Fits Into GRC
Compliance ties Governance and Risk together by:

- Verifying that policies and controls meet external requirements  
- Supporting audit readiness and evidence collection  
- Demonstrating alignment with major frameworks  
- Highlighting gaps and driving remediation  

This section shows readiness for real‑world compliance and audit responsibilities.
