# ISO 27001 Annex A Control Mapping

## Purpose
This document maps organizational controls to ISO 27001:2022 Annex A controls. It demonstrates alignment with organizational, people, physical, and technological security requirements.

## Scope
This mapping covers all four Annex A control domains:
- A.5 Organizational Controls
- A.6 People Controls
- A.7 Physical Controls
- A.8 Technological Controls
