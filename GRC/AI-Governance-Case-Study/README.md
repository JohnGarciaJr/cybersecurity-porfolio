# AI Governance Case Study

This project is a comprehensive, documentation‑driven analysis of a high‑impact AI system, designed to demonstrate practical skills in AI governance, risk assessment, and responsible AI practices. It mirrors the structure and expectations of real-world Responsible AI, GRC, and AI Risk teams.

The case study applies industry frameworks including:
- NIST AI Risk Management Framework (AI RMF)
- Microsoft Responsible AI Standard
- OECD AI Principles
- Common GRC methodologies (risk registers, control mapping, oversight planning)

## 📂 Project Structure

The project is organized into six sections, each reflecting a core component of AI governance:

### **01 — System Overview**
Defines the AI system, its purpose, stakeholders, data sources, and intended vs. out‑of‑scope uses.

### **02 — Risk Assessment**
Includes a detailed risk register, impact analysis, and realistic harm scenarios aligned with NIST AI RMF.

### **03 — Controls & Mitigations**
Maps risks to controls, outlines mitigation strategies, and connects governance controls to established frameworks.

### **04 — Model Card**
Documents model purpose, data, evaluation metrics, limitations, and ethical considerations.

### **05 — Policy & Governance**
Contains a Responsible AI policy, transparency report, and human oversight plan.

### **06 — Final Summary**
Provides an executive-level summary of risks, controls, and governance recommendations.

## 🎯 Purpose of This Project

This case study demonstrates the ability to:
- Evaluate AI systems through a GRC/IAM governance lens  
- Identify and assess AI risks (bias, privacy, misuse, security, over-reliance)  
- Develop actionable controls and oversight mechanisms  
- Produce audit-ready documentation  
- Communicate complex AI risks to technical and non-technical stakeholders  

## 🧩 Skills Demonstrated

- AI Governance & Responsible AI  
- Risk Assessment & Risk Register Development  
- IAM & Access Control Considerations  
- Policy Writing & Governance Design  
- Model Documentation (Model Cards)  
- Compliance Alignment (NIST, OECD, Microsoft RAI)  
- Ethical & Societal Impact Analysis  
- Executive Communication  

## 📌 How to Use This Case Study

Each folder contains a standalone artifact that can be reviewed individually or as part of the full governance package.  
This structure reflects how AI governance documentation is typically organized inside enterprises, consultancies, and regulated industries.
