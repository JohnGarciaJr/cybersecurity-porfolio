# Governance Controls Map  
## AI Governance Case Study — Facial Recognition Access Control System

This Governance Controls Map links system risks and implemented controls to major security and AI governance frameworks. It demonstrates alignment with industry standards and provides traceability for audits, compliance reviews, and responsible AI oversight.

---

## Frameworks Included

- **NIST AI Risk Management Framework (AI RMF)**  
- **NIST 800‑53 (Rev. 5)**  
- **ISO/IEC 27001:2022**  
- **IAM Governance & SOX-Aligned Controls**
